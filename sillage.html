The Idea

Fragrance is personal. The tools to collect it should be too.

Sillage is built for the collector who tracks their bottles by house, by note, by season — and wants a place worthy of the obsession. Not a database. Not a social feed. Something that feels like the fragrances it holds.

What Sillage Does

Four things, done precisely right.
01

Collection, curated

Every bottle you own or have tried, beautifully organized. Tag by house, note pyramid, season, occasion — and finally have the overview your collection deserves.

02

Wear logs & reviews

Log each wear with notes on sillage, longevity, and mood. Build a personal fragrance diary that sharpens your nose and makes every future purchase more intentional.

03

AI scent matching

Tell Sillage the occasion, the weather, the feeling — and it surfaces exactly what to reach for from your collection. Like a knowledgeable friend who knows every bottle you own.

04

A community of noses

Follow reviewers whose taste you trust. Share your own takes publicly. Discover bottles you'd never have found through search — only through someone's honest, considered opinion.
